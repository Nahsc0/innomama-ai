# Nigeria administrative boundaries

Source: geoBoundaries gbOpen, Nigeria ADM1, boundary ID NGA-ADM1-27671186. Original boundary source: GRID3. Boundary year represented: 2022.

Pinned download: https://github.com/wmgeolab/geoBoundaries/raw/9469f09/releaseData/gbOpen/NGA/ADM1/geoBoundaries-NGA-ADM1_simplified.geojson

The app rounds coordinates and projects the simplified geometry into SVG paths. These changes are for display, not a legal definition of boundaries. State grouping follows Nigeria's three northern geopolitical zones; FCT is a territory, shown separately. Facility points are invented and must not be used for navigation or referral routing.
